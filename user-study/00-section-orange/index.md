In this section, we will go through the typical workflow of trying to understand two-dimensional embeddings using Orange.

## Instructions

Before starting each of the quizzes, please open Orange, download the data for each quiz, and load it in your Orange workflow.

To answer the questions in the following quizzes, you are welcome to use any tool inside Orange, although we do recommend using the widgets outlined in the introduction. You can also download and use this [sample workflow](https://file.biolab.si/visual-explanations/user-study-sample-workflow.ows) we prepared ahead of time.

Once you have completed all three quizzes, please fill out the questionnaire at the bottom of the page. The questionnaire is short and anonymous and will ask you regarding your experience using Orange for interpreting embeddings.
